# Packing Template

Standing checklist, started 2026.09.28. Copy the relevant parts into each trip's packing list and trim what doesn't apply. Outfit counts come from the trip's day-by-day plan, the way `OneDrive/Personal/Travel.xlsx` counts them (Business, Business Casual, Casual, Evening, W/O, Golf).

## Clothes (count from the day plan)
- Business
- Business casual
- Casual
- Evening
- Workout sets (one per session in Megan's plan, plus a spare)
- Golf, if on the schedule
- Travel or driving outfits
- Sleepwear, underwear, socks (days + 1)
- Layers for the weather: fleece or sweater, jacket, rain shell
- Shoes: running, walking, business, casual, evening

## Workout
- Gym shoes, walking shoes
- Resistance bands (for hotel or bodyweight days)
- Heart rate strap or Whoop charger
- Water bottle

## Health and personal
- Medications and vitamins (days + 3 spare)
- Skin care and UV face care, sunscreen
- Teeth care (brush, floss, whitening if in the routine)
- Hair tools, makeup
- Glasses or contacts, sunglasses

## Work
- Laptop and charger, phone charger, power bank
- AirPods (car calls), phone mount
- Notebook
- Anything for in-person meetings: printouts, business cards

## Pierce
- Bed
- Food: daily amount x days + 2 days extra, in a sealed bin
- Treats, chews
- Toys (2 or 3 favorites)
- Bowls, plus a collapsible bowl for the car
- Leash, harness, collar with ID tag
- Poop bags
- Car setup: seat cover or hammock, seat belt tether
- Towel for wet or muddy paws
- Meds due during the trip (heartworm, flea and tick)
- Vaccination record on the phone (hotels and boarding ask)
- Water jug for the car
- Check each hotel's pet policy and fee before booking

## Car (EV road trips)
- Audi mobile charging cable
- Charging apps logged in and working: Electrify America, ChargePoint, Rivian
- Route file (`OneDrive/Personal/Michigan Trip.docx` for Michigan)
- Emergency kit, tire inflator
- Snacks and water for the day
- Audible downloaded ahead, for offline listening

## Before leaving
- Appointments: nails, hair
- Mail and packages held
- Thermostat, lights
- Plants and home check
