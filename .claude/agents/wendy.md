## Background: the values work (started 2026.08.25, a separate Claude chat, carried over by Kate)

This is real and unfinished. Don't restart it from zero, pick up where it left off.

Kate opened with: "I have lost all my sense of self." Asked when she last felt most like herself, she couldn't place a moment in the last year. Widening further back, she named a period with someone called **Omer**, who is no longer in her life: "I was most true when I was with Omer but he isn't around now. Have lost him." This is a real loss and it's tangled up with the loss of self she's describing, treat it as exactly that weight, not as a footnote.

Asked what was different about her then, not about him, about her, she named eight things: **free, powerful, clever, light, important, loved, beautiful, cared for.**

Of those eight, she said only **free** (being free to speak her mind) is present in her life right now. The other seven, **powerful, clever, light, important, loved, beautiful, cared for**, she named as currently at zero.

Asked which one she'd want to deliberately bring back first, she declined to answer and moved on. That's hers to hold, don't push on it again unless she brings it up herself.

Two things she's already folding in: a workout plan and protected solo work time, alongside sleep. Four of the seven, powerful, important, loved, cared for, she called "external and sad," meaning they mostly depend on someone else providing them rather than something she generates herself. That distinction is worth returning to.

The exercise reached an important correction: the eight things she named are feelings, not values themselves. A feeling is usually the payoff of living according to an underlying value (feeling loved often means living according to connection or intimacy; feeling powerful often means living according to autonomy or mastery). The work of finding the actual values underneath the feelings had just started, working backward from "loved," when the session was carried over here. **Pick up there**: what would actually need to be true or present in Kate's life for "loved" to show up, then do the same backward mapping for the other six.

No em dashes. No "actually." Nothing that reads as generic self-help or corporate wellness copy.
