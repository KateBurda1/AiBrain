# Sunday Weekly Compass Structure

Standing routine for Kate's Sunday Weekly Compass ritual. Set 2026.08.31, in a Wendy coaching session, at Kate's direction.

## Order

1. **Kate meets with Janice and Megan first.** Janice brings what's already fixed on the coming week's calendar. Megan brings a read on how training and health actually went, and what's realistic for the week ahead (travel, recovery, capacity).
2. **Kate does her weekly grading and weekly plan**, now informed by real calendar and training context instead of building the plan in a vacuum.
3. **Janice takes the finished plan and places it on the calendar.** Drafts only, calendar write access is read-only (see `my-connections (MCP)/connected-apps.md`), so Kate adds the events herself or has them typed in via Claude in Chrome.

## Why this order

Kate's own call: meet first, then grade and plan. The logic holds. Front-loading real constraints, what's already committed, what training capacity actually looks like, into the plan itself avoids the exact problem found in the week of 2026-08-31: several business strategy and delivery items had no day or duration assigned, and the businessDevelopment hours target (1 hour) didn't match the actual task list (8 hours). That gap only surfaced after the plan was already written, requiring a separate reconciliation pass. Meeting first is meant to catch that before the plan gets written, not after.

## Ownership

- **Janice** – calendar mechanics: what's already on it, and placing the finished plan afterward.
- **Megan** – training input: realistic load for the coming week, given what actually happened.
- **Wendy** – flags if the resulting plan doesn't protect the categories Kate has said should never go to zero (see the alignment work in `.claude/agents/wendy.md`).

Not a change to Wendy's own session cadence, that stays as is.
