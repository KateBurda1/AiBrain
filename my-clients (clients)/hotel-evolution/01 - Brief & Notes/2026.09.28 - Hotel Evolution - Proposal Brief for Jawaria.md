## The five sections

### 1. Work in progress

What's in motion right now.

Think about:

- For each item: what it is, where it stands, what's left, and who's waiting on whom
- What does Neel need to hear here so he knows we're on top of it?
- Is anything stuck on their side? Say so, kindly and plainly.

### 2. Discussed and on deck

Work we've talked about and agreed is coming, but haven't started.

Think about:

- Where and when was each one discussed? A date or meeting makes it real.
- What order should these go in, and why?
- Is anything here really a "nice to have" that should move to section 3?

### 3. Work that needs to be done

This is where I most want your thinking. You've talked about groupings for this work. Build them.

Think about:

- What are the 3 to 5 buckets? Name them the way Neel would understand them, not the way we'd label them internally.
- What's the business reason behind each bucket? What happens for Hotel Evolution if it gets done, and what does it cost them if it doesn't?
- Which bucket goes first, and why that one?
- What are you seeing that Neel hasn't asked for yet but should?

Write your buckets and reasoning down yourself first. Then take them to Chat to pressure-test.

### 4. Hours to date and work not yet paid

Where we are against the previous contract.

Think about:

- What the old agreement covered: hours, scope, rate, term
- Hours performed against that, by month or by project, whichever reads cleaner
- Work done outside the original scope
- What's been invoiced, what's been paid, and what's done but not billed

Keep this factual and clean. No apologizing, no padding. Show your math so I can check it. How we handle the unpaid balance (bill it, credit part of it, or fold it into the new agreement) is my call, but give me your recommendation and why.

### 5. Project outline, deliverables and investment

The go-forward plan.

Think about:

- Phases or workstreams, what each one delivers, and rough timing
- What's in scope, and just as important, what's out
- How we track hours and report them from here on, so we don't end up here again
- How work gets added (a simple change process)
- The investment: what structure fits this work best? Retainer, project fee, or a hybrid? Recommend one and tell me why.
