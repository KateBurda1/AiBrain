## Kate Burda & Co Launches Ignite, an Agentic SaaS Tool Built for Revenue Strategy

Kate Burda & Company has launched Ignite, an agentic SaaS platform designed to close the gap between detecting a market shift and executing a commercial response. The launch marks the firm's first software product after a decade of advising executive teams on sales performance and revenue strategy.

### From Consulting Thoughtware to Operational Software

For the past 10 years, Kate Burda & Company has developed frameworks and methodologies used by leadership teams to strengthen revenue strategy. That body of work, built through direct client engagements, now forms the foundation of Ignite. The platform translates advisory frameworks into an automated, agentic system that any commercial team can run without waiting on consulting cycles.

The core problem Ignite addresses is timing. Teams frequently identify a shift in the market months before they are positioned to act on it. By the time a revenue roadmap is built and a sales and marketing playbook is operationalized, competitors may have already moved. Ignite is built to compress that window.

### What Ignite Does Across the Revenue Cycle

Ignite operates across three connected functions that mirror how commercial teams plan and execute:

- **Trend detection:** The platform scans for signals that indicate a coming disruption or opportunity in the market, surfacing what Kate Burda & Company describes as a "quake" — a shift that appears on the horizon before it registers broadly.
- **Revenue roadmapping:** Once a trend is identified, Ignite generates a structured revenue roadmap. Rather than starting from a blank page, teams receive a directional plan built from the pattern recognition embedded in the platform.
- **Commercial playbook deployment:** Ignite produces the sales and marketing playbook needed to pursue the roadmap. This includes the tactical steps across both functions, designed so teams can move from strategy to execution without the typical lag.

### Addressing the Operationalization Gap in Sales Performance

The months-long delay between insight and action is not a strategy failure — it is an operationalization problem. Most teams know a market change is coming but lack the infrastructure to build and deploy a response at speed. Kate Burda & Company designed Ignite specifically to address that gap, using agentic AI to handle the structuring work that previously required extended consulting engagements.

Sales performance depends on more than knowing what to do. It depends on how quickly a team can organize around a new direction and get into market. By automating the translation of market intelligence into revenue strategy and then into executable playbooks, Ignite is intended to reduce the time from signal to action from months to a materially shorter cycle.

### A Decade of Advisory Work Behind the Platform

The launch of Ignite represents a shift in how Kate Burda & Company delivers its methodology. Rather than limiting access to firms engaged in direct advisory work, the SaaS model makes the firm's approach available to a broader set of commercial teams. The platform encodes 10 years of revenue strategy work into a system that runs continuously, rather than episodically.

Ignite is positioned for sales and marketing leaders who need to move faster than their current planning infrastructure allows — particularly those operating in markets where the competitive window following a trend shift is narrow.

### About Kate Burda & Company

Kate Burda & Company is a revenue strategy and sales performance firm with a decade of experience advising executive teams. The firm works with commercial leadership on identifying market opportunities, building revenue roadmaps, and developing the sales and marketing playbooks needed to act on them. Ignite is the firm's first agentic SaaS platform, extending its methodology into a continuous, software-driven format.
