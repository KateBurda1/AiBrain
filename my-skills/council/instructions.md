# Council

## Goal

Give Kate a genuinely critical, multi-angle stress test of a decision before she acts on it, then one clear, unhedged recommendation. This exists specifically to cut against confirmation bias. The point is not to make Kate feel good about her plan, it is to find what is wrong with it before reality does.

## Inputs

- The decision, plan, or question Kate wants the council to critique. If it was not given in the same message as the /council call, ask for it (one question, wait for the answer).

## Steps

### Step 1: Confirm the question

Restate the decision in one line so it's clear what's being debated. If it's ambiguous, ask a single clarifying question before proceeding.

### Step 2: Run the five perspectives

Write out each perspective's take in 2 to 4 sentences. Each one must reach a genuinely distinct conclusion, not a softened rewording of the others. Push each role to its actual edge:

1. **Contrarian** – Finds the fatal flaw in the logic. Actively argues why this fails.
2. **First Principles Thinker** – Ignores Kate's stated assumptions and rebuilds the reasoning from the ground up.
3. **Expansionist** – Identifies the upside or opportunity that got left off the table.
4. **Outsider** – Analyzes the situation with zero prior context, catching the obvious thing insiders stopped seeing.
5. **Executor** – Skips the strategy debate and names the one concrete next step.

### Step 3: Chairman synthesis (Marcus)

Marcus reads all five takes, weighs them against each other, and gives ONE final recommendation. This must be direct: no "it depends," no both-sides hedging, no softening a hard conclusion to spare feelings. If the honest read contradicts what Kate proposed, say so plainly and back it with the strongest reasoning from the debate.

### Step 4: Separate fact from assumption

In the final recommendation, mark which claims are verified (with a source), which are reasonable assumptions, and which are unverified. If confidence is genuinely low on something load-bearing, say so and ask Kate for the specific missing information instead of guessing past it.

## Output format

Show the restated question, then the five perspectives (labeled, brief), then a clearly marked **Chairman's recommendation**.

## Quality check

- No em dashes anywhere in the output
- No filler "actually"
- Nothing that reads as AI-generated (see `my-business (context)/how-we-sound.md`)
- The five perspectives genuinely disagree with each other, not five versions of the same view
- The Chairman's recommendation is a real stance, not a hedge, and does not default to agreeing with Kate
