# Meeting Notes to Actions

## Goal
Turn messy, unstructured notes into a clear summary with decisions made, actions assigned, and deadlines set. Something you can send to the team or refer back to.

## Inputs
- The raw notes (pasted text, bullet points, voice memo transcript, or description of what was discussed)
- Who was in the meeting?
- Any specific follow-ups the user already knows about?

## Steps

### Step 1: Read and organise
Read through the raw notes. Identify:
- Decisions that were made
- Actions that need to happen
- Who is responsible for each action
- Any deadlines mentioned
- Open questions that still need answers

### Step 2: Write the summary
Structure it as:

**Meeting:** [what it was about]
**Date:** [today's date or date mentioned]
**Attendees:** [who was there]

**Decisions**
- [Decision 1]
- [Decision 2]

**Actions**
| What | Who | By when |
|---|---|---|
| [Action item] | [Person] | [Deadline] |
| [Action item] | [Person] | [Deadline] |

**Open questions**
- [Anything unresolved]

**Notes**
[Any other context worth capturing]

### Step 3: Review
- Are all actions assigned to a specific person?
- Do all actions have a deadline (even if approximate)?
- Are decisions clearly stated, not buried in notes?
- Is anything important missing from the raw notes?

## Output format
Display the summary in the conversation. If the user wants it saved, save to the relevant client folder or `my-work (outputs)/content/other/`.

## Quality check
- Every action has a name and a date
- Decisions are clear and unambiguous
- Short enough to skim in 30 seconds
