# Write a Follow-Up

## Goal
Write a warm, non-pushy follow-up message that re-engages the person without making them feel chased. Gets a response without burning the relationship.

## Inputs
- Who are you following up with?
- What is the context? (quote sent, enquiry not replied to, lapsed customer, etc.)
- How long has it been?
- Any specific angle or offer to include?

## Steps

### Step 1: Pull context
Read `my-business (context)/my-voice.md` for how the owner actually writes. If it still has square-bracket placeholders, use `my-business (context)/how-we-sound.md` instead.
If the person is a known client, check `my-business (context)/our-clients.md`.

### Step 2: Choose the right angle

**Quote sent, no reply (3-7 days):** Light check-in. No pressure. "Just wanted to make sure that landed in your inbox."

**Quote sent, no reply (7-14 days):** Add a reason to act. "Wanted to let you know our schedule is filling up for [month]."

**Enquiry went cold:** Acknowledge the gap. "I know things get busy. Just wanted to check if you are still thinking about [project]."

**Lapsed customer (3-6 months):** Personal and warm. "Been a while since we caught up. How is everything going with [their business/situation]?"

**Lapsed customer (6+ months):** Reintroduce value. "We have been doing a lot of [relevant work] lately and thought of you."

### Step 3: Write the message
- Subject line: specific, not generic. Never "Just checking in" or "Following up."
- Opening: acknowledge the gap or context naturally.
- Body: one short paragraph. Give them a reason to respond.
- Close: one easy question they can reply to quickly. Make it simple to say yes.
- Keep it under 100 words. Short follow-ups get more replies.

### Step 4: Review
- Does it feel like a human wrote it, not a CRM?
- Is there zero pressure?
- Is there one clear, easy question to respond to?
- No banned phrases from how-we-sound.md?

## Output format
Display the message for review. The user copies and sends it themselves.

## Quality check
- Under 100 words
- No "just checking in" or "following up" or "circling back"
- One easy question at the end
- Sounds human, not automated
