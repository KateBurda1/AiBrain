# Reply to an Enquiry

## Goal
Produce a ready-to-send reply to a customer enquiry that sounds like you wrote it. Includes your pricing, availability, and a clear next step.

## Inputs
- The enquiry (pasted text, forwarded email, or description of what the customer asked)
- Any specific instructions ("offer them 10% off", "we're booked until next week", etc.)

## Steps

### Step 1: Understand the enquiry
Read what the customer asked. Identify what they want, any urgency, and what information they need.

### Step 2: Pull context
Read `my-business (context)/my-voice.md` for how the owner actually writes. If it still has square-bracket placeholders, use `my-business (context)/how-we-sound.md` instead (and mention that /learn-my-voice would make drafts sound more like them).
Read `my-business (context)/how-we-sound.md` for brand rules and banned words.
Read `my-business (context)/what-we-sell.md` for pricing and services.
If the customer is a known client, check `my-business (context)/our-clients.md`.

**Match the relationship, not just the voice.** If email is connected, search the sent folder for 3-5 past emails the owner wrote to this same person and read them before drafting. People write differently to a long-time client than to a brand-new enquiry, and the owner's own history with this person is the best guide there is. Match that register. If there is no history with this person (or no email connected), use the voice profile's examples and lean slightly more polite than casual.

### Step 3: Draft the reply
- Open with something warm but not generic. No "Thank you for your enquiry."
- Answer their actual question directly.
- Include relevant pricing or availability from what-we-sell.md.
- End with one clear next step (book a call, come in, reply with details, etc.)
- Keep it under 150 words unless a longer response is needed.
- Match the tone in how-we-sound.md exactly.

### Step 4: Review before showing
- Does it sound like the business owner wrote it?
- Is there a clear next step?
- No banned words or phrases from how-we-sound.md?
- **Check the never-rules.** my-voice.md and how-we-sound.md list things the owner never writes (a sign-off they never use, phrases they hate, greetings they skip). If the draft breaks one, rewrite it once before showing it. A draft that breaks a never-rule is not ready.
- Would you send this without editing?

## Output format
Display the reply in the conversation for review. The user will copy and send it themselves.

## Quality check
- Sounds human, not like a template
- Answers the actual question
- Has one clear call to action
- Under 150 words
