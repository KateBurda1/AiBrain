# Write an Ad

## Goal
Produce ready-to-use ad copy that stops the scroll, speaks to the customer's pain point, and drives action. Complete with multiple hook options.

## Inputs
- What are you advertising? (offer, service, event, promotion)
- Who is it for? (target audience)
- Any specific details? (price, dates, location, limited availability)
- What platform? (Facebook, Instagram, Google, etc. Default: Meta/Facebook+Instagram)

## Steps

### Step 1: Pull context
Read `my-business (context)/how-we-sound.md` for tone and voice.
Read `my-business (context)/what-we-sell.md` for offer details.

### Step 2: Write the ad
Produce all of these:

**3 hook variations** (the first line people see, each taking a different angle):
- Hook 1: Pain point lead ("Tired of [problem]?")
- Hook 2: Result lead ("What if you could [outcome]?")
- Hook 3: Direct offer lead ("[Specific offer] for [audience]")

**Primary text** (the main body, 2-3 short paragraphs):
- Open with the strongest hook
- Describe the problem the audience faces
- Present the offer as the solution
- Include social proof if available (results, numbers, testimonials)
- End with urgency if relevant (limited spots, ends Friday, etc.)

**Headline** (under 7 words, punchy):
- Clear benefit or offer

**Call to action:**
- One specific action (Book now, Send us a message, Click to learn more)

### Step 3: Review
- Does the hook stop the scroll?
- Is the pain point real and specific?
- Does it sound like the business, not a marketing agency?
- Is there one clear CTA?
- No banned words from how-we-sound.md?

## Output format
Display all copy in the conversation for review. If approved, save to `my-work (outputs)/content/ads/`.

## Quality check
- 3 distinct hook angles
- Primary text under 150 words
- Headline under 7 words
- One clear CTA
- Sounds like the business owner, not a copywriter
