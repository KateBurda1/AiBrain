# Write a Post

## Goal

Produce a ready-to-publish social media post that sounds like you, stops the scroll, and drives engagement. Matches your brand voice and the platform it is for.

## Inputs

- What platform? (Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, etc.)
- What is the topic or angle?
- Any specific points, offers, or calls to action?

## Steps

### Step 1: Read the brand voice

Read `my-business (context)/how-we-sound.md` and note the tone for social media specifically.

### Step 2: Draft the post

Write the post following these rules:

**Hook:** First line must stop the scroll. Lead with a bold statement, question, or pain point.

**Body:** 3-5 short paragraphs or punchy lines. One idea per line. Mix short and medium sentences for rhythm.

**Close:** One clear takeaway or call to action.

**Platform adjustments:**
- Instagram: emoji is fine, include a CTA, no hashtags unless asked
- LinkedIn: more polished, thought-leadership angle, no emoji overload
- Facebook: conversational, community-focused

### Step 3: Review

Check that:
- The hook is strong enough to stop scrolling
- The tone matches `my-business (context)/how-we-sound.md`
- No banned words or phrases are used
- The post is the right length for the platform

## Output format

Display the post in the conversation. If approved, save to `my-work (outputs)/content/social/` using the standard file naming format.

## Quality check

- Would you post this on your own business page without editing?
- Does the hook make you want to keep reading?
- Is the CTA clear?
