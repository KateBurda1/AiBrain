# Ask for a Review

## Goal
Write a short, personal message asking a happy customer to leave a review. Not cringey. Not pushy. Just a genuine ask from someone they already trust.

## Inputs
- Who is the customer? (name)
- What work did you do for them?
- How did it go? (any specific results or feedback they gave?)
- Which review platform, and the link? Google is the most common for local businesses, but it could be Facebook, Trustpilot, a marketplace, or an industry site. Ask if you do not know. Use whatever link they give, or skip the link if they have none.

## Steps

### Step 1: Pull context
Read `my-business (context)/how-we-sound.md` for tone.
If the customer is a known client, check `my-business (context)/our-clients.md`.

### Step 2: Write the message
- Open with something personal about their specific job or experience. Not "Dear valued customer."
- Mention the specific work you did. Make it feel like a real person remembering a real job.
- Explain why reviews matter in one sentence. Keep it honest. "It really helps other people find us" is better than "It would mean the world to us."
- Ask directly. "Would you mind leaving us a quick review?" Name the platform if you know it ("a quick Google review", "a review on our Facebook page").
- Include the review link if provided.
- Keep it under 80 words. Shorter messages get more reviews.
- No pressure. "No worries if you are too busy" or similar.

### Step 3: Review
- Does it feel personal, not templated?
- Is the ask clear and direct?
- Is it under 80 words?
- Would you feel comfortable receiving this message?

## Output format
Display the message for review. The user copies and sends it themselves (text, email, or DM).

## Quality check
- Under 80 words
- Mentions their specific job or experience
- One clear ask
- Not cringey or over-grateful
- Sounds like a mate asking a favour, not a corporation
