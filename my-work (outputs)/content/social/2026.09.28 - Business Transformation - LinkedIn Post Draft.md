## A note on the link placement

LinkedIn's algorithm tends to reduce reach on posts with an outbound link in the caption itself. Two ways to run this:

- **Link in the caption (above):** simplest, most direct, matches what you asked for. Some reach tradeoff.
- **Link in the first comment instead:** post the video with just the first two lines as the caption, then add the "Read the report" line as the first comment right after posting. Slightly better reach, one extra step.

Went with the caption version above since that's the direct ask. Say the word if you'd rather run the comment version instead.
