## The problem

**It is not a strategy failure. It is an operationalization problem.**

Most leaders see the shift coming. Then the strategy travels from the leadership team to the sales, marketing, and revenue people at each property, and it lands as the same 30-60-90 day task list they have always run. Good people, good tools. The strategy gets lost on the way down.

## What Ignite does

**Agentic, because it does the structuring work. Continuous, not episodic.**

- **Spots the quake.** A shift in demand, a segment, a feeder market, or a competitor's move, before it hits your numbers.
- **Builds the roadmap.** Built on a decade of revenue strategy work, not a blank page.
- **Deploys the playbook.** Deployed to the field, ranked by revenue and profit together.

**Plenty of AI makes the task list faster. Ignite decides what belongs on it,** then tracks which moves show up in the number, so the owner conversation runs on proof. It sits above your RMS, never in place of it.
