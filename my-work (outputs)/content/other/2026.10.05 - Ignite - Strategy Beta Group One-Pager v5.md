## The problem

**Busy isn't a strategy.**

Talented teams, good tools, a budget, a forecast, and a 30-60-90 list for every hotel. Then the market outruns the forecast and the year turns into reacting. Nobody dropped the ball. Planning was built for budgets and task lists, so great teams drown in activity, starved for impact.

| | |
|---|---|
| **3 months** | at best, from spotting a shift to acting on it. Results take longer. |
| **30-60-90** | day task lists in the field, the same as they have always been |
| **15.5 hours** | per hotel, at a management group, on a plan that was never used |
| **$1.3M to $2M** | estimated missed revenue in one 100-room asset in a tertiary market |

---

## What Ignite does

**Agentic, because it does the structuring work. Continuously, not once a year.**

- **Strategy.** Spots the quake, a shift in demand, a segment, a feeder market or a competitor's move, before it hits your numbers.
- **Focus.** Builds the revenue roadmap and ranks every move by revenue and profit together. Fewer moves. Bigger ones.
- **Timing.** The playbook lands with every hotel's sales, marketing, and revenue people, and stays current as the market shifts.
- **Proof.** Shows the impact of each action, so owners see what your team did, apart from what the market did.

**More dashboards won't fix it. Neither will a once-a-year plan.** Dashboards add reports. The plan retires to a shelf by spring. Ignite hands you decisions.
