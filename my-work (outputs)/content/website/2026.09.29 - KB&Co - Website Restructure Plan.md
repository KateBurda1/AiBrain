# kateburda.com Restructure Plan

Prepared by Maxell for Kate Burda

September 29, 2026

## The short version

Your instincts are right on almost every point. The site has eight menu items telling about five stories, so things repeat. We can get to six menu items, show every blog post, and put your best graphic (Where You Win) on the page it was made for. The design stays: same header, colors, fonts, hero style, card layouts and carousel. We are moving rooms around, not rebuilding the house.

## What is doubled up today

- **Insights and Explore are the same blog, twice.** Insights shows the 6 newest posts with images. Explore shows 10 posts with no images, plus a Tweets box. You have 22 posts, so 12 of them never show up anywhere. Your 2016 to 2020 pieces (Point of Inspiration vs. Point of Sale, the Tri-Fecta, Account-Based Marketing) are invisible.
- **Origin and Who We Are both tell Kate's story.** Same headshot on both, same "28 years, national and multi-national companies" message, just in different words.
- **An old Contact Us page is still live next to Connect.** Connect is your contact page and part of the brand (Origin, Path, Connect). But an older /contact-us/ page still exists, and the "connect with us" link on Path sends people there instead of to Connect.
- **Case studies live in the wrong spot.** The three case studies sit as small tiles at the top of Path, while the page called Where We've Been has no case studies at all. It has a slide carousel and the association logos.

Eight items down to six:

| Origin | Origin | Absorbs the Who We Are bios (Kate and Tristan) |
| Path | Path | Becomes the method page, built around Where You Win |

| Explore | (retired) | Redirects to Insights |
| Who We Are | (retired) | Content moves to Origin, and the client list moves to Where We've Been |
| Where We've Been | Where We've Been | Becomes the case study and results page |

New order (Kate's call, 2026.09.29, Insights moves up): Home, Origin, Path, Insights, Where We've Been, Connect. Who we are, how we work, how we think, the proof, then talk to us. The proof sits right before Connect, so the last thing a buyer sees before reaching out is results.

## Page by page

### Origin: the people

Keep your founder letter at the top exactly as it is. Below it, add the Who We Are content: your credentials list and Tristan's bio, using the same headshot card layout. This retires the Who We Are hero you do not like, the backpacker on the mountain, without losing anything.

You floated moving Who We Are into Path. I would split it instead. The bios are about people, so they belong with your origin story. The client names and results (Marriott, Hyatt, IHG, Abbott, Boehringer Ingelheim) are proof, so they go to Where We've Been. Path stays clean as the "how we work" page. If you still want some of it on Path, the client logo strip is the one piece that would work there too.

This is the best news in the audit. The Path page text already describes Where You Win, word for word. Right now that story is told with the prism graphic, when the graphic that shows it properly sits in your slide library.

1. Fix the broken CSM link, point the Path "connect with us" link to Connect, and fix the typos (quick wins, nothing else depends on them)
2. Build the Where You Win web graphic and rebuild Path
3. Rebuild Where We've Been with the case studies
4. Move the bios to Origin
5. Merge the blog pages and switch Tweets to LinkedIn
6. Update the menu and set the redirects last, so nothing points at a page that has not moved yet
