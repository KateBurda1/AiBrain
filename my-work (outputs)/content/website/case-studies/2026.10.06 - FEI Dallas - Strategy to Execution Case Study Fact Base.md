## 1. Before state: how FEI Dallas planned and grew

### How they planned
- The last formal strategic plan in the files is from 2014-15, done with The Richards Group. Source: `CF/Strategic Planning/Richards Group 2014 Strategic Planning/`.
- At the August 2025 board meeting, KB&Co's research summary said playbooks, documented decisions, routine handoffs and quarterly reviews "are missing, impacting continuity". It also cited over-reliance on key or legacy individuals and onboarding and committee transitions that needed more structure. Source: `CF/Board - Sponsors/FEI 2025 Board Meeting V5.pptx` (Aug 28, 2025), Continuity & Governance slide.
- Leadership turns over every year. Past president Jim Howe, in his planning interview: committees are turned over "to different people every year", and "we succeed year after year in spite of ourselves". Source: `CF/Strategic Planning/Ella Inputs/Jim Howe Interview.docx`. Internal interview, see Section 6.
- The March 2026 alignment report says that before the plan, the chapter ran "an acquisition strategy with no IMP [Ideal Member Profile] filter and no funnel. Activity is high; conversion data is zero." Source: `CF/Strategic Planning/Outputs for Strategic Plan/FEI Elevate Strategic Alignment Overview.docx` (March 2026, marked Confidential).

### How they grew membership
- Growth came mainly from word of mouth and member referrals. In the 2021 member survey (a university project, not KB&Co work), members said they were introduced to FEI mostly by word of mouth. Source: `CF/FEI Surveys/2021/FEI Dallas Presentation for Board Meeting.pptx`.
- The Insight Intelligence Report (Feb 2024) said traditional outreach works: "the conversion ratio of potential member to member campaign eclipses marketing outreach by more than 25%." Source: `CF/Deliverables/FEI Insight Intelligence Report.docx`.
- Outreach before KB&Co fell into three or four streams: newsletters (Sharla and National), social posts about new members and events (Frontier Marketing), renewals (PropFuel, with National sending its own renewals too), and member-to-prospect asks. The report found it consistent but "very 'we' focused", with "no sense of community" or personalization in renewals. Source: same report. The V4 edit adds "Nancy been following up on leads". Source: `CF/Pitch and Agreements/FEI Insight Intelligence Report V4.docx`.
- Digital marketing before KB&Co was run by Frontier Marketing at $1,087.50/month: 3 posts a week on LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram, plus LinkedIn ad management ($150) and ad budget ($300). Per KB&Co's later proposal, there was no outreach strategy, lead generation, CRM, reporting or advisory. Sources: `CF/Pitch and Agreements/Digital Marketing Service Proposal REVISED 07.01.25 - FEI Dallas.docx` (Frontier's own proposal), `CF/FEI_Digital_Marketing_Proposal.docx` (April 2026).
- Before the takeover, LinkedIn ads produced nothing: "Clicks: 0, Leads: 0, Spent: $0". Source: `CF/Marketing Mtg - Board/2025/FEI Marketing Outreach Nov.docx` (November 2025).
- Website: "No analytics access, no tracking, no landing page optimization." Messaging and brand voice were "not centrally managed". LinkedIn was "managed independently of overall strategy". Source: `CF/Marketing Mtg - Board/2026/Board Meeting Marketing Feb 26 .pptx`, "The Starting Point" slide. The April 2026 proposal adds: "Website redirected to national, no local analytics." Source: `CF/FEI_Digital_Marketing_Proposal.docx`.

### How they grew sponsorship
- Sponsorship was relationship-driven, with an "analog" offer. "Sponsors cannot measure what they get from FEI today... only as valuable as the person a sponsor happens to sit next to at a monthly meeting." Source: `CF/FEI Roadmap/FEI_Dallas_Q1_Q2_Roadmap_Board.pptx` (June 2026).
- The benefits were seats, banners, a looping slide at dinner and a printed directory listing. Source: `CF/2026 Proposals/FEI Sponsor Proposal KB&Co.docx`.

## 2. The strategic plan we built (Elevate)

### Phase 0: Diagnosis (late 2023 to early 2024)
- **Pitch:** "A Conversation of Possibility", prepared for Miguel Molina, Scott Smaistrla and Alice Elkins. The 5-step "Rapid Revenue Return" covers The Reveal, Insight to Execution, Install/Enhance/Elevate, Drive & Sustain, and Measure & Equip. Files are dated Sept to Oct 2023. Source: `CF/Strategic Planning/Strategic Planning Pitch and Agreements/FEI v7.pptx`. The executed version is `CF/Pitch and Agreements/Marketing Agreements Ongoing/FEI_KBurda_Marketing_Proposal Executed.pdf`, dated 2023-11-30.
- **Discovery (Jan 2024):** interviews with members (May Lee Harris, Tom Beitel, Mark Dendle, Jim Howe, new member Iain Grant, Ron Rushing) and the internal team (Jackie, Nancy, Sharla). Also conversations with PropFuel, National, Frontier and a competitive scan (CFO Leadership Council). Tech finding: National's robust system was 12-18+ months away, so the advice was a "simplified tech stack" now. Source: `CF/Marketing Mtg - Board/2024/FEI Update Jan 18.docx`.
- **Insight Intelligence Report + Findings & Recommendations (Feb 2024):** set the marketing goal ("50 new members annually along with decrease of attrition"). It recommended direct outreach to the 2,000 Metroplex executives by career stage, a shift from "us" to "them" messaging, leaving renewals to National, a CRM (ActiveCampaign), Sales Navigator, Dripify, and SEMRush/GA analytics. It also set roles: Nancy on admin and workflow, volunteers on final-stage outreach, and an advisor role. It said the proposed systems were "net neutral". Sources: `CF/Deliverables/FEI Insight Intelligence Report.docx` (2024-02-14), `CF/Deliverables/Findings and Recommendations.pptx` / `.pdf`.

### Phase 1: The Elevate strategic plan (June 2025 to March 2026)
- **Framework:** Roger Martin's "Playing to Win" cascade (aspiration, where to play, how to win, capabilities, management systems), adapted into KB&Co's ELEVATE framework. Its parts are North Star, Expected Outcomes, Positioning, Headwinds/Tailwinds, Ideal Member Profile, Where We Play, Core Capabilities, Continuity & Governance, and Winning Aspiration. Sources: `CF/Strategic Planning/FEI Playing to Win.docx` (June 2025), `CF/Strategic Planning/Strategy Sessions August/Elevate Framework.docx`.
- **Sprint plan:** July 11 to mid-August 2025: kickoff, stakeholder interviews, market and competitive scan, draft, a one-day workshop, then a board-ready plan. Sources: `CF/Strategic Planning/FEI Roadmap.docx`, `CF/Strategic Planning/Elevate Planning.docx`.
- **Method as reported to the board:** "15 In-depth interviews with the FEI Board, members, and sponsors", a "Detailed market scan", "Two collaborative workshops, real-time synthesis, consensus voting", and "15+ hours interviews, 40+ hours of research". Every point reflects a steering committee or board decision: "a 'by us, for us' plan". Source: `CF/Board - Sponsors/FEI 2025 Board Meeting V5.pptx` (prepared for the Aug 28, 2025 board meeting).
- **Engagement agreement for this phase:** dated Aug 13, 2025. Length "June 2025 - August 2026". Signed for FEI by Tom Beitel. Source: `CF/Strategic Planning/SOW Strategic Plan/FEI 2025 agreement_kB co.pdf`. Assets from the phase included a brand book, monthly meeting videos and a landing page. Source: `CF/Strategic Planning/SOW Strategic Plan/Invoicing 2025.xlsx`. Video scripts are in `CF/Videos/`.
- **Launch:** the plan called for a chapter launch on Sept 8, 2025, presenting the manifesto and first video at the chapter dinner. Source: `CF/Board - Sponsors/FEI 2025 Board Meeting V5.pptx`, timeline slide. **Not confirmed in files that this happened as planned.**

### What the plan decided
Source for all of this: `CF/Strategic Planning/FEI Decision Points- one pager.pdf` (Mar 2026), `CF/Strategic Planning/FEI Dallas Strategic Planning 2025.docx`, `CF/Board - Sponsors/FEI 2025 Board Meeting V5.pptx`.
- **Winning aspiration:** "FEI Dallas elevates and connects finance leaders, building a dynamic community where innovation thrives, growth accelerates, and collaboration shapes the future of North Texas." Tagline: "Your Legacy Belongs Here."
- **Positioning:** redefine "premier" as innovative, bold and trailblazing, keeping a warm, welcoming core. Board deck data point: 20+ Dallas organizations use similar language.
- **Competition reframed:** the real competitor is the executive's time. The goal is "Return on Time" and "the most valuable room in Dallas".
- **Strategic goals:** membership of 450 with an 85% retention rate; a cost-neutral income statement by 2029; recognition as trusted and best in class for forward-thinking leaders. Note: the Nov 2025 planning doc says 95% retention by 2029, while the Mar 2026 one-pager and alignment report say 85% (the alignment report and board guidance say "by 2030"). **Confirm with Kate which is final.**
- **Ideal Member Profile:** core is $100M to $2B revenue and 500 to 4,000+ employees. Emerging leaders are $25M to $100M and 300 to 1,000+ employees (the Aug 2025 deck says 150 to 500 employees; confirm).
- **Personas:** Visionary Connector (C-level legacy, "builders of bridges"), Strategic Advancer (rising star, next gen), Community Catalyst (trusted community builder).
- **Four initiatives:** (1) Activation & Member Engagement, (2) Member Experience, (3) Operational Efficiency & Strategy, (4) Brand Awareness.
- **Required capabilities:** a single source of truth (project management across all committee work), operating playbooks/SOPs, dedicated resources assigned to Elevate actions, and technology enablement.
