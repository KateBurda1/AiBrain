# KB&Co Search Gap Research: Impact, Path, and What's Missing

**Prepared by:** Scout, 2026.10.06
**Question from Kate:** How should we treat the Impact and Path pages, and what are we missing that people search for?

---

## Headline

People do not search in our vocabulary. "Hotel revenue strategy consultant," "hospitality revenue strategy," "revenue strategy consulting" and "account based marketing for hotels" all show zero or unmeasurable US volume. The demand sits in plainer words: **hotel consultant, hospitality consulting firm, revenue management consulting, ABM consulting, go to market strategy consulting.** The site never uses the word "hotel" or "hospitality" in a heading, and the four Impact blocks are tiles with almost no body text, so Google has nothing to match those searches to.

**Recommendation in one line:** keep Impact as the proof and Path as the method, but turn the four Impact sub-pages into real service pages (copy first, case study tiles below), add one hospitality industry page, and add an engagement model plus FAQ. That is six pages, four of which already exist.

**What the numbers say**
- **Hospitality is the bigger, easier pool.** Low difficulty (7 to 15) on "hotel consulting services," "hospitality consulting firm" and "hotel revenue management consulting." The big firms (HVS, PKF, CBRE) own feasibility and appraisal; almost nobody owns "commercial/revenue growth for hotels."
- **ABM consulting is the sleeper.** Difficulty 8, and a $69 CPC means advertisers pay real money for these clicks. We have an ABM case study shipping today.
- **"Revenue strategy" alone reads as revenue management** to hotel searchers, as our positioning doc already warns. Pair it with "consulting" and "hotel."
- **Don't build a page for "strategy to execution."** 720 searches but difficulty 89 and mostly students and HBR readers. Use it as a heading phrase on the existing Strategy to Execution page, not a target.

## 4. Recommendation: Impact and Path

**Impact (/what-we-do/): keep it, change what's underneath.**
- Keep the hub as the proof page ("Standing Apart, by Design"). It works as a brand page.
- The problem is the four sub-pages. Right now they are tile grids, so they can't rank for anything. **Turn each into a service page:** H1 with the search phrase, 400 to 700 words (who it's for, the problem, what we do, how the engagement runs, 3 to 5 FAQs), then the case study tiles underneath as proof. Same URLs, so no redirects and no lost links. Every comparable firm does it this way: service first, proof below.
- Add a short intro paragraph to the hub that says "hotels, resorts, management companies, DMOs and associations" in plain words.
- Minor: the URL says /what-we-do/ while the menu says Impact. Leave the URL alone (it has history); just make sure the page title names the services.

**Path (/pathway/): keep it as the method page, and add how we actually work.**
- Path is a trust and conversion page, not a search page. Nobody searches "performance path." That's fine.
- What it's missing is what every B2B comparable shows: **the steps, the deliverables, rough timeline and a starting point.** Today Path explains the philosophy but never mentions The Reveal, Insight to Execution, the Insight Intelligence Report or the retainer step. Add that section (see page 6) and link it from Path.
