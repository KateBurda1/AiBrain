## Intro

You already have a plan. Most teams do. The trouble usually isn't the plan. It's the gap between the plan and what happens on Tuesday.

We close that gap. Every engagement follows the same path, our Rapid Revenue Return, built so you see results early and keep them long after we've gone.

## Step 1: The Revenue Reveal

**See where the money is, and where it's slipping away.**

We start by sitting in the tent with you. Over four to six weeks we interview your team one on one, join your revenue, sales and marketing meetings, spend time onsite, and look at your business from the outside in: through the eyes of your customers, and their customers.

We look through four lenses:

- **Science and strategy:** who you target, which segments make you the most money, and whether your budget follows them.
- **Process and customer engagement:** how leads come in, how your team sells, and where business falls through the cracks.
- **Talent:** the skills, structure and roles you need to win.
- **Performance and metrics:** what you measure today, and what you should be measuring.

**What you get:** the Insight Intelligence Report, plus your data loaded into Ignite. What we found, why it matters, and what to do about it, in plain language your whole leadership team can act on.

---

## Step 2: Insight to Execution

**Turn what we found into a plan your team will run.**

We build the plan with you, not for you. Segment by segment, we set the strategy, the budget, the targets and the actions, and we put a name next to every one.

**What you get:** the Vision-to-Value Roadmap. Your strategy, the steps to get there, the owners, and the measures that tell you it's working.

From there, we can stay alongside you as you put the plan to work:

- **Install, Enhance and Elevate:** we help roll out the strategy, programs and tools, and train your team to run them.
- **Drive, Coach and Sustain:** we coach your leaders and build the rhythms (pipeline reviews, scorecards, weekly huddles) that make results repeatable.

---

## FAQ

**How long does it take?**
The Revenue Reveal takes four to six weeks. Insight to Execution usually follows over the next four to eight weeks. Ongoing coaching is set up as a monthly partnership, sized to what you need.

**Who from my team is involved?**
Your leadership team and the people closest to revenue: sales, marketing, revenue management and operations. We keep the time we ask of them focused and short. Most of the heavy lifting is ours.

**What does it cost to get started?**
Every engagement is sized to scope. Tell us where you want to go, and we'll show you what it takes to get there. Let's talk about yours.

**Do you only work with hotels?**
Hospitality is where we started and where much of our work is: hotels, resorts, management groups and destination organizations. The same path works for associations and B2B companies with a revenue team and a plan that isn't turning into results.

**Do you just hand us a report?**
No. A report on a shelf doesn't move the number. We stay with you through execution and measure the results with you.

**What's Ignite?**
Ignite is our agentic platform for revenue strategy. It keeps the plan and the daily work connected, so every action points at the number. Many clients use it alongside the work we do together.
