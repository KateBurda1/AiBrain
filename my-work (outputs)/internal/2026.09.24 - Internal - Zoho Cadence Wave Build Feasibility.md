# Zoho Cadence Wave Build — Technical Feasibility Check
2026.09.24 — for Kate, re: 12-topic ZoomInfo intent cadence build-out

## Short answer

Yes, "waves" is buildable — but not the way it's currently sequenced. Don't publish a cadence with only Touch 1 built and then add Touch 2 later. Build all 5 touch steps up front (placeholder copy is fine, exactly like the 11 topics already sitting in Zoho), publish once, and only ever *edit* step content from then on. That sidesteps the Draft-flip risk entirely, because editing an existing step's copy is a content change, not a structural change — it doesn't require re-adding the step.

## 1. What happens to enrolled contacts when a published cadence flips to Draft

Reasoning from the confirmed behavior (new step → auto-Draft, Draft → no new enrollments/no new sends):

- Their **position in the sequence is very unlikely to reset**. Step-add-triggers-Draft reads as a safety/validation gate on the automation engine, not a wipe of enrollment records. Contacts keep their place.
- They **will stall** for as long as the cadence sits in Draft — any touch that comes due during that window doesn't fire.
- The open question is what happens on **republish**: does Zoho recalculate each contact's next-send time against the original schedule and resume normally, or does it treat every overdue step as "due now" and fire a backlog all at once? That distinction matters a lot — a burst-send to already-warm prospects looks sloppy and could trip spam thresholds.

**This is not fully knowable from stated behavior alone — flag for a live test** before trusting it in production: pick one already-live topic (Business Transformation or Market Expansion), let a test contact sit mid-sequence, add and remove a harmless step to force the Draft flip, republish, and watch whether that contact's next touch fires immediately or waits for its scheduled date.

## 2. Recommended build pattern (avoids the risk instead of testing around it)

Reject "publish after Touch 1 only, add touches later" — it guarantees the Draft flip 4 times per topic, once per topic × 12 topics = 48 avoidable Draft cycles, each one an unknown-blast-radius risk to whoever's mid-sequence at the time.

Reject "publish after 2+ touches" — same problem, just fewer flips. Any plan involving *adding* steps to a live cadence keeps the risk on the table.

**Recommended: build all 5 steps up front, publish once, edit-in-place from there.**
- Matches what's already true for 11 of the 12 topics (steps exist, only copy is placeholder) — no new pattern to learn.
- Write real Touch 1 copy before publish so people start receiving something real immediately.
- Touches 2–5 can stay as placeholder at publish time — the cadence's own 7-day gaps give a full week of runway to swap in real copy for Touch 2 before the earliest cohort reaches it, then another week for Touch 3, and so on.
- Editing a step's content (not adding/removing a step) should not trigger the Draft flip — worth a one-time confirmation on the first content swap, but low risk given it's a much narrower action than adding a step.

## 3. Concrete session-by-session order

**Phase A — finish structure, get Touch 1 live everywhere (target: first ~1.5 weeks, ~6 sessions at 4/week)**
1. Session 1: Finish Site Visit Targeting — it already has Touch 1; add steps 2–5 (placeholder) + build/bind the Custom View + auto-enroll Cadence, publish with Skip Existing Records.
2. Sessions 2–6: Build Custom View + Cadence + all 5 steps (Touch 1 real copy, 2–5 placeholder) for the remaining 9 topics, ~2 topics/session:
   - Session 2: Sales Performance Management, Sales Intelligence
   - Session 3: Revenue Intelligence, Sales Transformation
   - Session 4: B2B Marketing, Digital Marketing
   - Session 5: Business Development, Revenue Management
   - Session 6: Revenue Management Software (+ buffer/QA pass on all publishes from this phase)
   - Publish each topic as soon as its 5 steps exist — don't batch-wait for all 10.

**Phase B — content waves across all 12 topics (remaining ~4.5 weeks, ~18 sessions)**
- Week-by-week, swap placeholder → real copy for one touch number across all 12 topics before that touch comes due for the earliest-enrolled cohort:
  - Weeks covering Touch 2, then Touch 3, then Touch 4, then Touch 5 content, ~4–5 sessions per wave (roughly 3 topics/session).
- No new steps added at any point in Phase B — content edits only, so no Draft flips, no stalled contacts, no risk to anyone already mid-sequence.

## Bottom line
The plan works if you flip the build order: steps first (all 5, placeholder is fine), publish once per topic as soon as Touch 1 copy is real, then spend the 6 weeks writing content into slots that already exist. Verify the republish-resume behavior once on a live topic before leaning on it — but with this build order, you should never need to rely on it anyway.
