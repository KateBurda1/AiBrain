## 5. Build note for Tristan: the overlap problem

Because so many contacts carry multiple intent tags at once, enrolling strictly by "contains this topic" criteria will put the same person into three or four cadences running at the same time. That reads as spam even though each individual cadence is fine on its own.

**Recommended fix — enroll each contact into exactly one cadence, based on their rarest tag.** Rarer tags are the more specific signal about what that person is actually dealing with; common tags like Digital Marketing are closer to a default label most enterprise contacts pick up.

Priority order for assignment (rarest first, meaning: if a contact matches more than one, enroll them in whichever of these comes first on this list):

1. Track 1 — Revenue Intelligence / Sales Intelligence (51 combined)
2. Track 3 — Revenue Management / Revenue Management Software (182 combined)
3. Track 2 — Market Expansion (110)
4. Track 4 — Business Development (165)
5. Track 5 — Sales Performance Management (262)
6. Track 6 — Business Transformation (392)
7. Track 7 — Sales Transformation (453)
8. Track 8 — B2B Marketing (355)
9. Track 9 — Digital Marketing (693)

In Zoho Cadence terms: build the enrollment criteria for Track 9 (Digital Marketing) to explicitly exclude anyone who also matches Tracks 1 through 8. Same logic cascading down. This can be done with an AND NOT clause on `ZI_Intent_Topic` in the enrollment criteria, or with a suppression list built once and referenced by every cadence below it in priority. Either way, no contact should be sitting in two active cadences at once.

## 6. General build notes (apply to every track below)

- **Module:** Contacts (not Leads). Enrollment criteria uses `ZI_Intent_Topic contains "<topic name>"`.
- **Sender:** Kate, from her own inbox, not a shared/sales alias.
- **No pricing anywhere in these sequences.** The $15,000 floor comes up on a call, never in outreach copy.
- **One ask per email.** Every email below ends in exactly one question. Don't let a rep or automation bolt on a second ask.
- **`[First Name]` and `[Company]`** are the only merge fields used. Nothing here invents a fact about any contact, everything is written to be true for anyone on the list.
- **Stop conditions:** any reply pulls the contact out of the cadence automatically (standard Zoho Cadence behavior). Set this explicitly if it isn't the default.
- **Cadence length:** 4 touches over 12–14 days. Long enough to not feel like a blast, short enough that it doesn't linger past relevance.

## 7. Summary for Tristan

- **9 cadences to build**, covering 11 of the 12 topics (ABM excluded for now, 0 contacts).
- **Build the enrollment exclusion logic in Section 5 first**, before turning any cadence live. Without it, contacts with multiple tags (which is most of them) get hit by several cadences at once.
- **Every cadence is 4 steps over 12–14 days**: Email, Email, LinkedIn, Email (breakup). Timing is staggered slightly (Day 1/4/7-9/12-14) so all nine don't feel mechanically identical if the same person somehow sees more than one over time.
- **No pricing, no hard CTA beyond a 15-minute conversation**, anywhere in this spec. That's intentional and shouldn't be edited in during the build.
- Kate reviews and approves final copy before anything goes live. This document is the draft, not the send.
