## Angles for Maxell, Maya and Steph

1. **"Budget season is the problem."** Owners are writing 2027 plans right now, in a spreadsheet, and the plan will be stale by March. A Revenue Growth System replaces the annual plan with one that keeps listening. Timely through November.
2. **"Your platform should compound what it learns."** Noble said it out loud this summer. Use the idea, not the attribution, in public content: when a win at one hotel stays at that hotel, the portfolio is leaving money on the table. This is the cross-portfolio pattern engine in plain words.
3. **"Every new hotel restarts the clock."** Crescent, PM, Raines, Midas and Sandpiper all added hotels this year. Each takeover or opening is a new team learning the commercial plan from scratch. That lag is where the $1.3M to $2M per asset goes.
