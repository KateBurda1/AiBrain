## Step 5: Messaging (draft, for Kate's review)

**Core message**
Ignite is the agentic platform for revenue strategy. It closes the gap between seeing a market shift and your hotels acting on it, and it shows your owners exactly what moved the number.

**Three pillars: See it. Act on it. Prove it.**
1. **See it first.** Spot the shift in demand, a segment, a feeder market or a competitor's move before it hits your numbers. (Proof: STR and CoStar benchmarking; the "quake.")
2. **Act on it now.** Ignite turns the strategy into a revenue roadmap and a ranked playbook in the hands of the sales, marketing and revenue people at every property. Continuously, not once a year. (Proof: Revenue Roadmap, Ignition Score, playbook engines; a quarter becomes a much shorter cycle.)
3. **Prove it.** See which moves showed up in revenue and profit, and walk into the owner meeting with facts. (Proof: impact by action; the 16:1 story; $1.3M to $2M estimated missed in one 100-room asset.)

**Headline options**
- A. Lead the market instead of reacting to it.
- B. You see the shift in a week. Your hotels act on it in a quarter. Ignite closes the gap.
- C. Strategy that reaches every hotel while it still matters.

**Emphasis by buyer**
- Dawn Gallagher (Crescent): more from the tools and team you already have. Your strategy runs in the field, not a 30-60-90 list.
- Kerry Ranson (Raines): stop reacting. A plan and forecast that stay current, and margin protected.
- Steven Nicholas (Noble): proof of what the operator did, separate from what the market did.

**Guardrails:** the gap is the system, never the people. Revenue strategy, not revenue management; sits above the RMS. No speed number promised. Fuse is not built yet.

### Step 5: Value proposition (draft, for Kate's review)

**Full statement**
For hotel owners and operators who have the data and the tools but still run last quarter's plan, Ignite is the agentic platform for revenue strategy. It turns a market shift into the few moves that matter at every hotel while they still matter, and it proves to owners which moves grew revenue and profit. Unlike a revenue management system that prices tonight, or a consulting engagement that delivers a plan once a year, Ignite runs all year and shows its return.

**The value, in three parts**
1. Time: the quarter between seeing a shift and the field acting on it collapses, so the opportunity is still there when the team arrives.
2. Focus: effort goes to the business worth the most, revenue and profit together, instead of the busiest.
3. Proof: leaders can show owners what they did, separate from what the market did.

**Short version**
The strategy you already have, in the field in time to matter, with proof it worked.
