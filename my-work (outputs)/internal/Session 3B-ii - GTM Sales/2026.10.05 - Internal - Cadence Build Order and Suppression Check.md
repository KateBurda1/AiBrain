### Fastest way to build it (Kate in Zoho, about 45 minutes)

This mirrors the Meeting Booked stop built on 10/1, so the pattern is already proven in this org.

1. **Make one checkbox** on Contacts (and Leads): "Ignite Beta Hold."
2. **One workflow rule** on Contacts, on Create or Edit: if Email contains any of the domains above, tick Ignite Beta Hold. This catches every future ZoomInfo arrival automatically.
3. **Tick the 11 existing records** by mass update (search each domain in Contacts, select all, Mass Update). The rule only fires on new edits, so the existing records need this once.
4. **One criterion on every auto-enroll view:** "Ignite Beta Hold is not selected." Views today: BT Auto-Enroll ZI Intent Topic, Sales Intelligence Contacts, ABM Auto-Enroll (ZI Workflow Source), the ABM - ZI filter, the three SVT Chose views, and Market Expansion's. Every new topic view gets it at Build.
5. **One un-enroll criterion on every live cadence:** "Ignite Beta Hold is selected," OR'd with reply and Meeting Booked, on ABM, ABM Auto-Enroll, BT Auto-Enroll Live, SI Auto-Enroll and the three From Site Visit cadences. That removes anyone who slips in before the view catches them.
6. **Upstream, in ZoomInfo:** add the six companies to the exclusion list on the intent and Site Visit Targeting workflows, so they stop syncing with topic tags at the source.
7. **Then retire the account-name and hp-hotels.com exclusions** on the BT and SI views, once the field is in place, so Noble Corp and Paramount contacts are not suppressed by mistake.

**Deadline:** before Revenue Intelligence switches on (Tue 10/13). If it is not done by Mon 10/12, Revenue Intelligence waits a week.
