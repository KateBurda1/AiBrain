## 1. What this changes from the first draft

The first draft gave every intent topic the same shape: two emails, one LinkedIn touch, one breakup email, four steps, done. That works for a single guess at what will land. It does not give Zoho Cadence anything to react to.

This version builds each track as a real branching engagement. Every contact gets the same first two emails. After that, the path splits based on whether they opened either one. Contacts who engage get a warmer, more specific sequence that assumes interest. Contacts who go quiet get a different angle entirely, not a repeat of the same message in a different font.

Ten tracks are built below: the nine from the original data pull, plus a tenth for Account-Based Marketing, built as a ready-to-use template even though zero contacts currently carry that tag. Section 10 explains why that track is worth building now instead of waiting.

Everything below is drafted. Nothing has been sent. Kate reviews and approves before anything goes live.

## 2. How the branching engine works

Every track follows the same skeleton. Only the words and the exact day numbers change per track.

**Stage 1, Entry Sequence (applies to every contact, no branching yet):**
- Step 1: Email, Day 1
- Step 2: Email, Day 4

**Stage 2, Branch check, Day 6:**
- Zoho Cadence branch condition: `Email Opened = Yes` on Step 1 OR Step 2 (either counts).
- If yes: contact routes to **Path A**.
- If no opens recorded by Day 6: contact routes to **Path B**.
- This is a one-time fork. Once a contact is on a path, they stay on it. They do not get re-evaluated against the other branch later.

**Path A, for contacts who opened at least one email (they are reading, just not replying):**
- Step 3: LinkedIn message, referencing the emails already sent
- Step 4: Email, a more direct ask paired with a proof point from Kate's track record
- Step 5: Breakup email

**Path B, for contacts with zero opens (the insight-led email format is not landing, so the format itself changes):**
- Step 3: Short, blunt one-line email. No story, no framework language, a plain direct question.
- Step 4: LinkedIn message, sent cold, no reference to the earlier emails since there is no evidence they were seen.
- Step 5: A longer-gap re-engagement email that also serves as the breakup. Different hook than everything before it. Last attempt, then exit.

**Universal stop condition:** any reply, on any step, on either path, pulls the contact out of the cadence immediately. This is standard Zoho Cadence behavior. Confirm it is switched on, do not assume it is the default.

**Fields needed for this build:**
- `Email Opened` (native to Zoho Cadence's open tracking, used as the Stage 2 branch condition)
- `Days Since Enrollment` (native day-count trigger, drives every wait step below)
- No new custom fields required. This runs on what Zoho Cadence already tracks.

**Merge fields:** `[First Name]` and `[Company]` only, same as the original draft. Nothing below invents a fact about any contact. Every line is written to be true for anyone on the list.

**No pricing anywhere in any step of any track.** This is top-of-funnel engagement copy, not a pitch with numbers attached. The floor comes up on a call, never before.

**One ask per message.** Every single step below, on both paths, ends in exactly one question or one request. No stacking a second ask underneath it.

## 5. Priority and overlap logic

Because so many contacts carry multiple intent tags, enrolling strictly on "contains this topic" would put the same person into three or four cadences running at once. Each cadence would be fine on its own. Together, it reads as spam.

**Fix: enroll each contact into exactly one cadence, based on their rarest tag.** A rarer tag is a more specific signal about what that person is dealing with. A common tag like Digital Marketing is closer to a default label most enterprise contacts carry.

Priority order for assignment, rarest first. If a contact matches more than one topic, enroll them in whichever track comes first on this list:

1. Track 10, Account-Based Marketing (0 today, reserve top priority for when this activates, see Section 10)
2. Track 1, Revenue Intelligence / Sales Intelligence (51 combined)
3. Track 3, Revenue Management / Revenue Management Software (182 combined)
4. Track 2, Market Expansion (110)
5. Track 4, Business Development (165)
6. Track 5, Sales Performance Management (262)
7. Track 6, Business Transformation (392)
8. Track 7, Sales Transformation (453)
9. Track 8, B2B Marketing (355)
10. Track 9, Digital Marketing (693)

In Zoho Cadence terms: build enrollment criteria for each lower-priority track to explicitly exclude anyone who also matches a higher-priority track above it. This can be an AND NOT clause on `ZI_Intent_Topic`, or a suppression list referenced by every cadence below it in priority. Either way, no contact should sit in two active cadences at once.

## 6. General build notes, apply to every track below

- **Module:** Contacts, not Leads. Enrollment criteria uses `ZI_Intent_Topic contains "<topic name>"`.
- **Sender:** Kate, from her own inbox, not a shared or sales alias.
- **Branch mechanics:** see Section 2. Every track uses the same Stage 1 to Stage 2 structure, then splits into Path A and Path B.
- **Cadence length:** roughly 15 to 25 days total depending on path, staggered slightly per track so all ten do not fire on identical day numbers if the same person somehow sees more than one over time.
- **Stop condition:** any reply exits the cadence immediately, on either path, at any step.
