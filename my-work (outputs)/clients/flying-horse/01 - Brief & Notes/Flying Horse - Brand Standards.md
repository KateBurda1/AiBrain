# Flying Horse Resort & Club: Brand Standards (working copy for agents)

Source: `2025.12.12 - Flying Horse - Brand Guidelines.pdf` (2026 Brand Guidelines, in this folder). Anything client-facing for Flying Horse uses THEIR brand, not KB&Co's.

## Colors (max 3 per layout, only these)
- **Copper, Pantone 876, #966A4D**: the hero color, used as much as possible. Otherwise reversed-out white, then gray or black.
- Flying Horse Red #591B16 (accent)
- Flying Horse Green #343B36 (accent, used as the dark background)
- Flying Horse Gray, Pantone 411, #5B5255 (accent, body text)
- Black #000000 (accent)
- Steakhouse Red #7D241E (Steakhouse materials only)

## Fonts
- Headlines and subheads: Brother 1816. **In Word and PowerPoint use Avenir or Calibri.**
- Body: Calluna. **In Word and PowerPoint use Times New Roman.**
- Lindsey Signature: only for headlines on amenity-specific pieces.
- Email signatures: Calibri.

## Logo rules
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "X".
- Never watermark, crop, recolor, flip or stretch the logo. No dark logo on a dark background, no light logo on a light one, and never on a busy background.
- The website has a white logo (FH-RESORT-Logo-RGB-White-Large.png, horizontal version too) for dark or photo backgrounds.

## Naming
- Always "Flying Horse Resort & Club" with the "&", never "and". "Flying Horse Resort & Club" replaced "The Lodge" in marketing materials (3/1/2024).
- Approved phrases: "Experience the unbridled majesty of Flying Horse", "Bold. Beautiful. Wildly Original.", "European-style clubhouse", "Award-winning steakhouse", "Championship golf courses", "Meet, Stay, Play", "AAA Four Diamond Hotel", "Take your next meeting or event to extraordinary new heights".

## Key facts for sales decks (from flyinghorseresort.com, Sept 2026)
- AAA Four Diamond. 102 rooms, suites and villas. Every guestroom is 500+ sq ft with a private balcony or patio.
- Weiskopf Course (the Club Course, used for Monday outside events): designed by Tom Weiskopf, opened 2005, views of Pikes Peak and the USAFA. Voted Best Golf Course in Colorado Springs by The Gazette in 2024, 2025 and 2026. Audubon certified.
- Flying Horse North (Tuesday events): designed by Phil Smith, 10 minutes away. Golfweek Top 200 residential courses 2024 to 2026.
- The Steakhouse holds the Wine Spectator Best of Award of Excellence (since 2023) and has an on-site butcher shop. Other outlets: Fortezza Italiana (hotel lobby), Tack Room (overlooks Hole 15), Pavilion (on course).
- Event rooms: Barolo (largest), Thomas Blake (Pikes Peak views, fire pit patio), Milano, Turin, the boardroom (16), Sienna (wine tunnel), the Rotunda (inside the Steakhouse).
- Key contacts: Mack Borowicz, PGA, Head Golf Pro; Sarah Leach (rooms); Shanna Hughes (catering); JoDee McGinnis.
