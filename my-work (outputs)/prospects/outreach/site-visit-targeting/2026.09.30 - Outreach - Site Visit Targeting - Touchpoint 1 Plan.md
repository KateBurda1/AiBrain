## The message

**Headline:** Three things we've been working on.

**Why a choice.** We don't know why their company came to the site, and the person we're writing to may not be the one who visited. So Touch 1 doesn't guess their problem. It hands over the work and lets them pick. Their click tells us what they care about, and it decides which thread Touch 2 follows.

**How it ties back to Stage 1:**

## What Zoho CRM can and can't do for the click routing (checked in Chrome 2026.09.30, nothing saved)

- **Cadence steps:** each step is only an Email, Task or Call. A step can wait on "the previous email is Opened / Clicked / Bounced / Replied / Unsubscribed / Sent / Not Opened." "Clicked" means any link. It can't tell which box was clicked, and a step can't tag a contact or enroll them in another cadence.
- **Workflow Rules:** they can only trigger on a Record action, a Date/Time field, Record Score, or Record Notes. There's no email-click trigger.
- **Enrollment:** cadences enroll either manually or from a Custom View. The existing "Content Downloaded" rules work because a form sets a field on the contact, a workflow fires on that field edit, and a Custom View picks the contact up. The same pattern works here once something sets a field when a specific link is clicked.
- **Starting at Touch 2:** a cadence starts everyone at Follow-up 1. To start clickers at Touch 2, each series needs a copy of its cadence without Follow-up 1 (for example, "Business Transformation - from Site Visit").
- **Status seen:** Site Visit Targeting is off and enrolls from a Custom View (150 records). ABM is on, with 89 enrolled manually. The others are off.

## Zoho Marketing Automation check (2026.09.30, nothing created or changed)

- **What's there:** Marketing Automation is active in Kate's Marketing Plus (org 934473073). There are no journeys yet.
- **It can react to one specific link.** A journey can run "Clicked on a specific link" for a chosen email and link, then take a CRM action. Journey CRM actions are Push Data (update existing CRM records, mapping fields), Create Task and Create Deal. Zoho's documentation says to put a wait before the click check, and a contact only goes down a click path once.
- **Tags don't sync.** Use a custom field instead, for example a CRM picklist "SVT Choice" with Climate, Lucky and ABM.
- **Blocker 1:** Marketing Automation isn't connected to Zoho CRM. The only connected apps are Zoho Analytics and Zoho Survey.
- **Blocker 2:** the Touch 1 email has to be sent from Marketing Automation, not the CRM cadence, so that Marketing Automation sees the click.

**The chain if we go this route:**

1. Connect Marketing Automation to CRM and sync the 56 SVT Approved contacts.

3. Push Data sets the CRM "SVT Choice" field.
