## 2. Buyer approach — confirmed 2026.09.27

**Addressee: CEO/President**, same as every other series (they still own the buy). **Message stays function-agnostic**, not narrowed to one department's language, since the account-level signal shows the whole exec bench watching together, not one searcher. Thinking/feeling at each stage carries both threads: the addressee and the watching committee (CFO, CTO, CMO, CHRO, etc.), each independently arriving at their own version of the same realization. This is documented as the standing approach for any topic where Zoho shows a committee instead of one clean persona — see `my-skills/intent-series/instructions.md`.

**Core theme, Kate's own words 2026.09.27:** "When I think of intelligence I think it is about data driven decision making." This is the thread for the whole series: are the decisions running the business actually backed by real signal, or by confident experience that sounds like data.

**Working differentiated-message candidate: "Right, or lucky?"** Kate confirmed she loves it (2026.09.27) and confirmed hooks can repeat across stages and series if they hold water. Built out below at Stage 1, footed against Sales Intelligence's own buyer logic on its own terms, not because it worked at Business Transformation's Stage 4. Different intent, different people, different reasoning each time — a placement that fit one series doesn't carry over to another just because it fit there. Whether it also fits at a later stage here gets tested independently against this series' own thinking/feeling, focus, and what-we-have when we get to that stage. It might. It might not.

**Other available hooks (not yet assigned to a stage):** "Bringing simplicity to your complexity" (Kate, 2026.09.27) joins the existing set — "Do less, make it mean more," the silent saboteur, the War Room, Outside-In Thinking. Test any of these against a stage's own footing when it comes up, same bar as everything else.
