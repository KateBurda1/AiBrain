## 2. Instigator, thesis and buyer approach, confirmed 2026.09.27

**Who is really searching.** Nearly the whole C-suite tagged at once reads as a company-level research surge landing on senior names. The CEO of Equifax isn't personally reading about sales intelligence tools. The real researcher is likely sales leadership, RevOps, or someone building a vendor shortlist.

**Instigators considered:**
1. **The number got harder to hit.** Pipeline thin, deals slower, cost to win rising. Reflex: "we need better data on who to go after." Matches the overlap tags and Kate's Chesapeake case. **Building on this.**
2. **Budget season and tool renewals.** Late September, 2027 plans and budgets being built, sales tool contracts renewed or replaced in Q4, every vendor pitching AI. **Building on this.**
3. **Leadership change** (new CRO/CSO auditing the stack). **Its own play later, via ZoomInfo Scoops.**
4. **Not buyers at all** (Equifax, Snowflake, iHeartMedia sell data or sell into this space). No message converts this. Noted, not designed for.

**Thesis (Kate, 2026.09.27):** it's about *what data should we build our budgets and plans on.* The reflex is to buy another sales intelligence tool, but those tools tell you who to call and what already happened. They don't tell you where next year's growth comes from, which accounts and segments are worth funding, or how big the gap is between the target and what the plan will deliver. That plays straight into Ignite, so **this series carries a great deal of Ignite.**

**Kate's precedent:** the Chesapeake case, where sales tools were "utilized after the stay had happened or post sale (i.e. Knowland Report)." Intelligence used to explain the past, not decide the next move.

**Buyer approach.** Addressee: CEO/President, same as every other series. Message stays function-agnostic, since the whole exec bench is watching. Thinking and feeling carries both threads: the addressee and the committee (CFO, sales, marketing, CTO), each arriving at their own version of the same worry.

**Hook:** "Right, or just lucky?" at Stage 1 and Stage 4, each footed on its own logic (confirmed by Kate).

**Proof for the final stage:** the 16:1 story, described for this series as **"an asset manager overseeing many hotels."** RLJ is never named. The framing mirrors BlackRock and Newmark, who are asset managers themselves. Boehringer Ingelheim, Medtronic, J&J, Abbott and Indeed are not written up and not approved for use in copy. Kate can speak to them live.

**Tone:** clever, thought-provoking, even cheeky.
**Look:** mostly black, white and greys, with KB's two brand colors used for impact: PMS 214 raspberry and PMS 675 berry. KB colors only (Kate, 2026.09.27).
**Timing:** budget season is the moment. Flagged to Maxell for the marketing calendar.
