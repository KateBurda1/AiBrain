## Touch 2 (template "Sales Intelligence Cadence - Touch 2"), FINAL, approved 9/29

**Subject:** Which customers are worth winning?

Hi ${Contacts.First Name},

Your tools know who to call. Your team knows how to win. Does your plan know which customers are worth winning?

Most leadership teams know more about their market than ever: contacts, intent signals, who's in-market this week. And next year's plan still starts with last year's number, plus a percentage.

The tools answer who to call tomorrow. They don't answer which customers drive profitable growth. Those aren't the same list.

One asset manager overseeing many hotels saw a 16 to 1 return and called it a win. Aimed at their least valuable customers, it needed 22 to 1 just to break even. Right number. Wrong customer.

I put together a five-minute check you can run before the budget locks: Worth Winning? (link "Worth Winning?" to https://kateburda.com/worth-winning/)

Which customers is ${Contacts.Account Name}'s plan built around?

Warm Regards,
Kate
