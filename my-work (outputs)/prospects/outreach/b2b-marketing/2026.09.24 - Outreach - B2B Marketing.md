## Zoho cadence: Track 8: B2B Marketing (355 contacts)

**Companies on this list:** AVI-SPL, Cognizant, Gartner, NetApp, WESCO, DXC Technology, JetBlue, Aramark, Staples, BlackRock, Republic National Distributing Company, iHeartMedia, Enterprise Products Partners, Agilon Health.

**The angle:** B2B marketing leaders live under constant pressure to prove marketing spend connects to revenue. That is a Foundation, Framework, Focus problem. Most marketing plans get built from tactics up instead of from revenue impact down.

### Entry Sequence

**Step 1, Email, Day 1**
Subject: Marketing spend that connects to revenue

Hi [First Name],

Most B2B marketing plans get built tactic by tactic: a campaign here, a channel there. The plans that move revenue get built the other way, starting from the revenue target and working back to which marketing investments earn their place.

That is the Foundation, Framework, Focus approach I use with clients. It is less about doing more marketing and more about doing the marketing that is provably worth it.

Is that a live question at [Company]?

Warm Regards,
Kate

**Step 2, Email, Day 4**
Subject: Outside-In Thinking, applied to marketing

Hi [First Name],

Most marketing strategy starts from the product and works outward. Better marketing strategy starts from the customer's customer and works backward. That single shift changes what gets funded and what gets cut.

If [Company]'s marketing strategy could use that lens, I would like to talk it through.

Warm Regards,
Kate
