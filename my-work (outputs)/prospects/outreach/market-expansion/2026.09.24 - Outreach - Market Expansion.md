## Zoho cadence: Track 2: Market Expansion (110 contacts)

**Companies on this list:** Booz Allen, Cognizant, Verizon, ConocoPhillips, Procter & Gamble, NVIDIA, Compass, ADP, Twilio, AECOM, Cracker Barrel, Estee Lauder Companies, Cargill, Scripps Health.

**The angle:** Expansion plans (new markets, new segments, new geographies) fail most often because they get built from the company's own product outward instead of from the new customer's world inward. That is the definition of Outside-In Thinking. Lead with it plainly.

### Entry Sequence

**Step 1, Email, Day 1**
Subject: The expansion plans that hold up

Hi [First Name],

Most market expansion plans get built from the inside out: here is our product, here is who else might want it. The ones that hold up get built the other way, starting from the customer's customer and working backward.

I call that Outside-In Thinking. It is the foundation of how I help companies expand without burning a year finding out the hard way what the market wanted.

Is market expansion on the table at [Company] right now?

Warm Regards,
Kate

**Step 2, Email, Day 4**
Subject: What "ready to expand" really means

Hi [First Name],

Being ready to expand and having a plan that tells you where to put the effort are two different things. The Revenue Reveal step of my methodology exists to answer the second one before anyone commits budget to the first.

Happy to walk through what that looked like for a recent client if it is useful.

Warm Regards,
Kate
