**Revenue Strategy, Years 2–3**

Kate's redirect after the first draft: less about reporting cadence and measurement philosophy, more about the actual arc from brand (Year 1) through marketing (early Year 2) into sales execution (Year 2–3), with Ignite as the throughline connecting each stage back to one revenue target instead of three siloed sets of metrics.

- **Every Year 1 baseline becomes a Year 2 target.** Sessions, followers, subscribers, listings, and length of stay aren't just reporting metrics, they're the inputs to a revenue case. Before Year 2 budget goes to Council, we translate what Year 1 proved into specific shoulder-season revenue and length-of-stay targets, not another round of awareness goals.

- **Year 2 turns the brand into a go-to-market plan, not just a media plan.** Take the priority visitor segments Year 1 research surfaces, map each to the channel and message that actually reaches them, and set a revenue outcome per segment, shoulder-season stays, off-peak bookings, group nights. That's the audience-to-channel-to-revenue chain Ignite runs on, not audience-to-impressions.

- **The site's own tools become the sales channel, not just the shop window.** Plan-your-trip and the business directory are already scoped in Element B. Year 2 turns them into an actual conversion path, direct links into lodging partners' booking engines, tracked so a campaign dollar can be traced to a booked stay, not just a session.

- **Group and meetings sales gets a real go-to-market motion.** The site already carries a meetings and groups page. Year 2 turns it from a page into outbound: a sales sheet, direct outreach to meeting planners and small conference organizers, timed to the off-peak windows Datafy shows need it most, actual sales execution, not another content pillar.

- **Trade and travel-partner outreach extends past what paid media alone can reach.** Where the Front Range paid campaign hits a ceiling, Year 2–3 adds a trade motion, tour operators, AAA, motorcoach and group travel, that reaches visitors outside the paid funnel entirely and gets priced and measured the same way as the rest of the program.
