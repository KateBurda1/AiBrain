# My Voice

This file is how YOU actually write, learned from your real sent emails. It is different from `how-we-sound.md`, which is your brand voice for ads and posts. This one makes your drafted emails sound like you, not like AI.

Built from 25 real sent emails read on 2026.08.25 (Outlook, Sent Items).

---

## How I open emails

Usually no greeting at all for a quick reply, straight into the point ("Thank you !", "Sweet. I appreciate the work.", "Can you try this?"). When there is an opener, it's short and plain: a first name with a dash ("Likhitha-"), "Good Morning.", or a name in the body ("Brian, just a quick note..."). Never "I hope this email finds you well" or anything close to it.

## How I sign off

**"Warm Regards,/ /Kate"** is the default sign-off for anything that isn't a one-line reply, both words capitalized. For quick back-and-forth replies, often no sign-off at all, just the name or nothing. Full signature block (name, title, phone, email, website) shows up on emails that open a new thread or go to someone less familiar.

## My usual length

Very short. Most real replies are one sentence, sometimes a sentence fragment. Even when explaining something, it's two or three short lines, not paragraphs.

## How I sound

Direct and plain-spoken, contractions throughout, occasional casual grammar ("if there is anyway to get the digital" instead of "any way"), that's normal for me, not a typo to fix. Double punctuation for emphasis ("Yep. That's it!!"). Rare, occasional emoji with people I know well (😊). No em dashes, ever.

## How I ask for things

Short, direct questions. No hedging, no "whenever you get a chance." Examples: "Can you try this?", "Do you want me to put these in AC?", "What is the meeting URL? I don't have it in the invite."

## How I handle bad news or saying no

Not much padding. State the situation plainly and move to the next step: "We are tied up prior?" (proposing an alternative time instead of just declining).

## Phrases I use a lot

- "Warm Regards," (sign-off)
- "Sweet."
- "Yep."
- Starting a reply with the person's name and a dash instead of "Hi [name],"
- "Just a quick note..."

## Things I never do

- Never opens with "I hope this email finds you well" or similar
- Never signs off "Best," or "Kind regards"
- Never uses em dashes
- Never pads a short answer with unnecessary context
- Never uses more than one exclamation mark pattern beyond "!!" for genuine emphasis, and even that is rare

## When replying to someone Kate has emailed before

Read a few of Kate's past emails to that specific person first and match that relationship's tone before falling back to this profile. A regular collaborator (like Tristan Carty or the Flying Horse team) gets terser, more shorthand messages than someone external or less familiar.
