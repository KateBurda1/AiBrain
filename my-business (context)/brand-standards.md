**Usage rules, from the style guide, verbatim intent:**

- **For full color printing, the logo must only be set in PMS 214, PMS 675, and PMS 425** — that specific three-color combination, not any single brand color chosen freely.
- For flat/single-color use, the logo may be set in any color from the Primary Palette below.
- Never alter the mark: no additions, deletions, transposing elements, spinning, skewing, or stretching.
- Never use a screen grab or a low-resolution export — always pull from the source files above.
- The Logotype and Monogram are standalone marks and should only appear alongside other supporting brand elements, not floating alone with nothing else branded around them.

## Typography

**Brand-approved faces:**

- **Freight Big** (serif) — for marketing collateral, sub-heads and announcements. Not for body copy.
- **Typonine Sans Pro** (sans-serif) — for headlines and body copy across all communication materials. Full weight range: Light, Normal, Text, Medium, Bold, each with an italic.

**Web / PC / practical substitutes**, per the style guide, for email and internal office communication where the brand fonts aren't installed:

- **Franklin Gothic** — Book, Medium, and their italics. Actual font files present: `URW Type Foundry - FranklinGothicURWBoo(k).ttf`, `...Boo Italic.ttf`, `...Med.ttf` — a redistributable clone of Franklin Gothic, safe to embed/install.
- **Times New Roman** — Regular, Bold, and their italics.

**Also present in the fonts folder, not named in the style guide text, status unconfirmed:**

- **Aileron** (full weight family, Thin through Black) — likely a free stand-in for Typonine Sans Pro's weight range where the licensed font isn't available. Treat as a practical substitute, not confirmed brand-approved.
- **Galyon** and **GANTIC** (web font files, `galyon-cufonfonts`, `gantic`) — identical files also found in Ignite's brand assets. These appear to be shared between the parent brand and the Ignite sub-brand, though neither is named in this style guide's text. Worth a direct check with Kate before treating either as brand-approved for Kate Burda & Company itself, as opposed to Ignite specifically.

## Relationship to CLAUDE.md's brand rules

`CLAUDE.md` already carries lightweight brand guidance (business name, website, tone: "warm but clear, polished, clever, intelligent but approachable," no em dashes, no "actually"). That's voice, not visual identity, and it stays there. This file is the visual specification, colors, type, logo usage, that CLAUDE.md doesn't cover. Both apply together; neither replaces the other.
