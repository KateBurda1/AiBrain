## Writing rules

- Short sentences. Get to the point.
- American spelling (color, organize).
- Active voice.
- Contractions are fine (we're, you've, it's).
- Lead with the point. No waffle.
- No em dashes, ever. Use a period, comma, or parentheses instead.
- Never use "actually."
- Nothing that reads as an obvious AI tell (see below).

## Words and phrases we use

- "Warm Regards," as a sign-off (both words capitalized), for anything beyond a one-line reply
- Framework language: "Vision-to-Value Roadmap," "Foundation, Framework, Focus," "Outside-In Thinking," "the customer's customer," "silent saboteur," "War Room," "Straight-Shot," "Do Less, Make it Mean More"

---

## Words and phrases we never use

- Corporate jargon in general (synergy, leverage, ecosystem, etc.). Kate specifically hates the word **"actually."**
- **No em dashes.** Ever, in any writing.
- No "AI tells": anything that reads as obviously AI-generated (stock phrasing, hedge-y qualifiers, overly tidy parallel structure, etc.). Writing should read like a sharp, witty human wrote it, not a model.
