**The mark:** a power-button symbol (circle with a vertical stroke through the top) in brand pink, wrapped in concentric ripple lines that fan out to a soft triangular silhouette. Wordmark is lowercase "ignite" in a heavy geometric sans.

**Naming, note the distinction:** "dark" and "light" in these filenames describe the **wordmark**, not the background. Dark = dark wordmark for light backgrounds. Light = light wordmark for dark backgrounds.

## Typography

From the fonts embedded in `IGNITE_BrandStyleGuide.pdf`:

- **GANTIC Regular** — display face used in the style guide
- **Galyon Book** — secondary face

Both are flagged as **needs confirmation**: the guide predates the current logo, so its type direction may have moved too. GANTIC's embedded metadata credits a Fiverr designer, which suggests a licensed one-off rather than a foundry face, so check licensing before using it in anything client-facing.

Where these are unavailable, use a clean geometric sans and say so, rather than substituting silently.
