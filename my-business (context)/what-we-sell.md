## How we work with clients

**Thoughtware** uses a signature methodology called the **"5-Step Rapid Revenue Return"** — "From Insight to Impact." It starts project-based, then shifts into a **retainer** at step 4:

1. **Revenue Reveal** — deep dive into the current state: what's working, what needs to be left behind, what needs to be built. *Deliverable: Insight Intelligence Report. This is also where the Ignite tool itself gets used.*
2. **Insight to Execution** — brings the insight of what great looks like, plus the execution plan to make it happen. *Deliverable: Vision-to-Value Roadmap.*
3. **Install, Enhance & Elevate** — implementation. "Let's go to work!" *Deliverable: implementation of strategy, tactics, programs.*
4. **Drive, Coach & Sustain** — how results become repeatable. **This is where the engagement converts to a retainer.** *Deliverable: sustainability program, maturation modeling.*
5. **Measure & Equip** — measuring results and KPIs, readjusting, continually moving to the next level. *Deliverable: benchmark scorecard, KPIs.*

Steps 1–3 are project-based (see pricing below); step 4 onward is retainer. Core differentiator woven through all steps: **"Outside-In Thinking"** — understanding "the customer's customer" rather than starting from your own product. Invoicing on the project phase is commonly split 50% at start / 25% at midpoint / 25% at completion, with a **"Bank of Hours"** for work beyond the agreed scope, billed hourly.

**How Kate actually prices it:** she estimates the hours a given engagement will take behind the scenes, then quotes it out by section of the Revenue Roadmap (i.e., by which of the 5 steps are in scope), rather than using one fixed rate card. This applies across the whole methodology, project phase and retainer phase alike.

**Ignite:** subscription SaaS once launched (see pricing below); currently running a capped, paid beta program. Also used as a tool inside Thoughtware engagements (Revenue Reveal step).

**2. Ignite — software platform (currently launching)**

An "agentic SaaS" — positioned as generative-AI-powered "Strategy as a Service" — that bridges revenue strategy and execution. Origin: started as an Excel model Kate built by hand to quantify the dollar gap between a company's revenue roadmap/vision and what it was actually doing to close that gap — often finding millions of dollars of gap. Covered in a USA Today press release: "Ignite Agentic SaaS Targets Revenue Strategy Gaps." Legal entity: Ignite Sequence, LLC (Texas). Tagline: **"do less. make it mean more."**

**Current product, straight from the live app** (igniteapp-rouge.vercel.app — this supersedes the descriptions below, which came from 2022–2025 marketing decks and are now dated):

The whole product is organized around **"The Ignite Equation"** and a core metric called the **Ignition Score**, which ranks segments, accounts, and even people (see Talent below). A built-in AI assistant named **Iggy** ("Ask Ignite..." command bar, ⌘K) surfaces insights and can be asked to generate/scan things throughout the app.

- **Home** — "What should I focus on right now?" A daily-focus dashboard: Current/Target/Gap/Trend revenue snapshot, top Ignition scores in scope, assigned actions.
- **Internal** (where money comes from, what it costs, what you keep):
  - *Revenue Roadmap* — a full revenue modeling workspace: worksheet grid, Ignition Ranking, Account Valuation (priority), Iggy-generated insights, and "Ripple Effect" scenario/revenue modeling.
  - *Cost of Acquisition*
  - *Aspirational Calculator* — where targets get set (feeds the Current/Target/Gap math)
- **External** (what's happening in the market, and what to do about it):
  - *Marketplace* — market signals, uploaded "STAR reports," and Iggy can scan for macro forces. Sub-pages: Market Impact, Competitive Landscape, Demand Pulse.
- **Execution** ("Straight Shot in, Strategies & Tactics out"):
  - *Go-to-Market* — three engines, each its own page: **Sales Engine** (priority accounts, Ignition-ranked pipeline, six-tab Account Plans), **Marketing Engine** (awareness switches, ignition-weighted ROAS, Reach/Reinforce/Respond/Retain motions), **ABM Engine** (six journey stages coordinating sales and marketing on priority accounts).
  - *Strategies* — segments ranked by Ignition Score; "Straight Shot" strategies and owned actions laddering up to revenue.
  - *Ignite Talent* — a people-intelligence module: behavioral competency assessment (not "mystery scores"), dual-sided (manager + team member) gap analysis that drives coaching conversations, with level bands (Underperforming / Developing / Experienced / Mastery). Ties competency directly to revenue/GTM outcomes via Iggy.
- **Report Builder** — custom reporting across the above
- **Fuse** — the underlying configuration layer: role weightings, competency matrices, cadence, roster, and scoping/visibility rules that everything else respects ("Fuse-scoped")

**Target customer (confirmed by Kate, 2026.08.25):** B2B. Core focus is **hospitality and hospitality-adjacent** — going after owners and developers who often hold other assets beyond the one property (so one relationship can open multiple properties/deals). **Startups** are a second core focus, served through the lighter **Spark** tier. **Pharma** is a market Kate is actively exploring expanding into next.

**Launch stage:** publicly announced via a USA Today press release ("Ignite Agentic SaaS Targets Revenue Strategy Gaps," XPR Media distribution, dated July 30, 2026) as launched. Kate separately confirmed (2026.08.25) the product is real and functional, not a mockup, but not yet ready for customers to use day-to-day. **Open tension to resolve: the public press release says "launched" in the past tense; internally it's pre-customer-ready. Press release positioning (source: USA Today/XPR Media, 2026.07.30): Ignite is framed as the firm's "first software product after a decade of advising executive teams," encoding that advisory methodology into an "agentic SaaS" that runs continuously rather than episodically. Three connected functions described:
- **Trend detection** — scans for market signals, surfacing what Kate Burda & Co calls a "quake," a shift appearing on the horizon before it registers broadly
