## Your story

Before founding the company in 2016, Kate did a lot of strategic planning work as a practitioner, including with Gaylord properties. Doing that work from the inside, she kept seeing the same pattern: a big gap between where a company said it wanted to go and what it was actually doing to get there, "random acts of activity" instead of a coordinated plan. She also kept seeing leaders reach for the same tired playbooks, doing what they'd always done rather than figuring out what actually needed to change. That observation is the founding insight behind the whole business: a relentless focus on revenue growth, and on what it actually takes for the commercial team (sales, marketing, and pricing) to close that gap. Kate has built proprietary IP in sales approach and marketing methodology from that work.

Ignite (the software) came later, growing directly out of that same insight: it began as an Excel spreadsheet Kate built by hand because, in her own words, "I couldn't find a solution for our clients, so I built it." It quantified the dollar gap between a company's revenue roadmap and its actual execution, often surfacing millions of dollars of gap. That tool has since grown into Ignite, the SaaS platform, now positioned as generative-AI-powered "Strategy as a Service."
