## HRI Lodging

Past engagement. **Management offices in New Orleans; the hotels themselves are in other markets, Philadelphia among them.** Confirmed by Kate 2026.08.29.

Source document: `Kate & Co/Client Files/1- Past Engagements/HRI/Final Documents for HRIL/HRIL Findings & Recommendations.pdf` (OneDrive), titled "HRIL Revenue Acceleration Findings & Recommendations."

**The hours story, verified from that document.** Kate's team counted the time the client's teams spent producing a marketing plan **that was never used**:

| Level | Time per hotel | Cost of time per hotel |
|---|---|---|
| Area level | 4 hours | $161.60 |
| Corporate DOS | 11.5 hours | $724.00 |
| **Total above-property** | **15.5 hours** | **$885.60** |

Above-property time only, so it undercounts rather than overcounts.

**The "540 hours" figure that circulates in older material is NOT in this document.** It may be a portfolio roll-up (15.5 hours across roughly 35 hotels lands near 540) but nothing states that. Do not use 540 until sourced. Use the per-hotel figures, which are stronger anyway because a prospect can multiply by their own asset count in the room.

**Never name HRI in client-facing material.** Same rule as RLJ: the story is a client miss. In external material they are **"a hotel management group"**, with no market or city. Note the deliberate difference from the 16:1 descriptor ("a hotel in a major leisure market"): both companies are New Orleans, so identical descriptors would let a reader merge them or triangulate. Keep the two descriptors distinct and do not harmonize them.

**Why this document matters beyond the numbers.** Written years before Ignite existed, it contains Kate's current positioning as a client diagnosis: "teams working hard and doing a lot, but no return on time spent"; "misalignment of resources and time, highest and best use"; "unable to see impact of campaigns or initiatives"; "you can't judge what isn't measured"; most profitable segments have no strategy or tactics; no synergy between sales, marketing and revenue. Its recommendation reads: **"a single system or source of truth that gives reports to different audiences for different purposes."** That is Ignite, specified in a client deck before it was built. The USA Today press release claims Ignite encodes a decade of advisory work into software, and this document is the dated receipt for that claim.
